# MELT-O HAM & CHEESE TAKOYAKI

#### This is a Group Final Project for Event-Driven Programming (EVD) and Business Analytics, created by Group 7 consisting of Four members.

## Overview
Melt-O Ham & Cheese Takoyaki is a proposed school food business that sells affordable ham and cheese takoyaki, offering a delicious, non-seafood snack for students and young professionals.

## Languages Used
- HTML
- CSS
- Python

## Description
Melt-O specializes in ham and cheese takoyaki, available in three serving sizes: 3 pieces for ₱30, 5 pieces for ₱45, and 8 pieces for ₱70. The business focuses on affordability, quality, and convenience, especially for students looking for budget-friendly snacks.
